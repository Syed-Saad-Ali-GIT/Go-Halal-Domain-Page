Executive Summary

This Cookie and Tracking Policy provides comprehensive information about how Go Halal ("we," "our," or "us") uses tracking technologies in our mobile application and website to collect, process, and utilize information about your usage patterns, preferences, and interactions with our services.

Key Points:

  • We use tracking technologies to improve your experience and app functionality
  • You have control over most tracking through device settings and in-app preferences
  • We prioritize user privacy and data security in all tracking activities
  • This policy complies with Australian Privacy Act, GDPR, and international standards

Comprehensive Definition of Tracking Technologies

Core Technologies

Cookies and Similar Storage Technologies:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary data stored during your app session
  • Persistent Cookies: Data that remains between app sessions
  • Local Storage: Information stored locally on your device
  • Cache Files: Temporary files that improve app performance

Mobile-Specific Tracking:

  • Mobile Analytics SDKs: Software development kits that collect usage data
  • Device Fingerprinting: Collecting device characteristics for identification
  • App Instance IDs: Unique identifiers for each app installation
  • Push Notification Tokens: Identifiers for sending notifications

Advanced Tracking Methods:

  • Behavioral Analytics: Tracking user interaction patterns
  • Performance Monitoring: Collecting app performance and crash data
  • A/B Testing Tools: Tracking different user experiences for optimization
  • Heatmap Analytics: Understanding user interface interactions

Comprehensive User Control and Privacy Options

Enhanced Device-Level Controls

Location Tracking Management:

  • iOS Users:
    • Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Go Halal
    • Choose: Never, Ask Next Time, While Using App, or Always
    • Precise Location toggle for exact vs. approximate location
  • Android Users:
    • Settings > Apps > Go Halal > Permissions > Location
    • Options: Allow all the time, Allow only while using app, Ask every time, Don't allow
    • Precise location controls in advanced permissions

Advertising and Analytics Controls:

  • iOS Advertising ID:
    • Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising > Personalized Ads (toggle off)
    • Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements (customize sharing)
  • Android Advertising ID:
    • Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization
    • Google settings for analytics data sharing

Advanced Data Retention and Management

Granular Retention Policies

Location and Geographic Data

  • Precise Location: Never permanently stored, processed in real-time only

Device and Technical Data

  • Device UUID: Retained while app is installed, deleted upon uninstallation
  • Performance Metrics: Retained for 12 months for optimization purposes
