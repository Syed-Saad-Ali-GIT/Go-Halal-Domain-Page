Content Submission Guidelines for Go Halal

Last Updated: 31st July 2025

Overview

Go Halal relies on community contributions to build a comprehensive, trusted database of halal products and restaurants. These guidelines ensure submissions are accurate, helpful, and appropriate for all users while maintaining the highest standards of reliability and Islamic compliance.

Product Submissions

Required Information

  • Product Name: Complete and accurate product name as shown on packaging
  • Brand: Manufacturer or brand name
  • Product Category: Food type (snacks, beverages, frozen foods, etc.)
  • Barcode Photo: Clear image of the barcode (UPC/EAN) - mandatory for verification
  • Ingredients Photo: Clear, readable image of complete ingredients list
  • Product Photo: Clear image of the product packaging/label showing front and relevant sides
  • Halal Certification Status: Specify certification body or mark "No visible certification"

Enhanced Photo Requirements

  • Resolution: Minimum 1080p quality for all images
  • Lighting: Well-lit photos with natural or bright white light
  • Focus: All text must be sharp and completely legible
  • Angle: Straight-on shots, avoid tilted or angled photos
  • Complete Coverage: Show entire ingredients panel, not cropped sections
  • Multiple Views: Include front, back, and relevant side panels
  • Background: Plain, contrasting background preferred
  • No Filters: Submit unedited, original photos only

Advanced Accuracy Standards

  • Physical Verification: Submit only products you have personally examined
  • Formula Changes: Report if ingredients differ from existing database entry
  • Certification Verification: Cross-reference visible halal marks with certifying body
  • Expiry Awareness: Note if this is a limited-time or seasonal product
  • Size Variants: Specify product size if multiple sizes have different ingredients
  • Uncertainty Declaration: Explicitly state any doubts about halal status

Restaurant Submissions

Required Information

  • Restaurant Name: Official registered business name
  • Complete Address: Full street address, suburb, state, postcode, country
  • GPS Coordinates: Latitude and longitude for precise location
  • Contact Information: Phone number and website (if available)
  • Cuisine Type: Primary and secondary cuisine categories
  • Halal Status: Fully halal, halal options only, or mixed menu
  • Certification Details: Certifying body name, certificate number, expiry date
  • Kitchen Status: Dedicated halal kitchen, shared kitchen, or unknown

Enhanced Restaurant Information

  • Operating Hours: Complete schedule including special holiday hours
  • Prayer Facilities: Availability of prayer space or direction indication
  • Alcohol Policy: Served/not served/BYOB policy
  • Delivery Options: Available delivery platforms and halal guarantee
  • Special Dietary Options: Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free availability
  • Price Range: Budget category ($ to $$$$)
  • Seating Capacity: Approximate number of diners accommodated
  • Parking Information: Availability and type of parking

Comprehensive Verification Requirements

  • Personal Visit: Submit only restaurants you have personally visited within last 6 months
  • Certification Verification: Photo of displayed halal certificate or direct confirmation with management
  • Menu Verification: Confirm halal items are clearly marked or entire menu is halal
  • Cross-Contamination Inquiry: Ask about kitchen practices and food preparation
  • Staff Confirmation: Speak with knowledgeable staff about halal procedures
  • Multiple Source Verification: Cross-check with other reliable sources when possible

Prohibited Content

Strictly Forbidden

  • Deliberate Misinformation: Knowingly false or misleading information
  • Duplicate Submissions: Multiple entries for identical products/restaurants
  • Spam Content: Irrelevant, repetitive, or automated submissions
  • Personal Data: Individual phone numbers, home addresses, private information
  • Inappropriate Media: Offensive, explicit, or copyrighted images
  • Commercial Promotion: Advertisements, affiliate links, or promotional content
  • Incomplete Data: Submissions missing mandatory required fields
  • Competitive Sabotage: False negative information about competitors

Quality Violations

  • Blurry Images: Photos where text cannot be clearly read
  • Partial Information: Cropped ingredient lists or incomplete data
  • Outdated Information: Submissions based on visits more than 12 months ago
  • Hearsay Submissions: Information not personally verified
  • Assumption-Based Status: Guessing halal status without confirmation

Enhanced Submission Process

Detailed Workflow

  1. Pre-Submission Check: Search existing database to avoid duplicates
  2. Information Gathering: Collect all required data and high-quality photos
  3. Self-Review: Verify completeness and accuracy before submission
  4. Initial Submission: Submit through secure app interface
  5. Automated Screening: System checks for completeness and duplicates
  6. Expert Review: Trained reviewers assess accuracy and Islamic compliance
  7. Verification Process: Cross-reference with official sources and databases
  8. Quality Assurance: Secondary review for complex or disputed submissions
  9. Decision Communication: Approval, rejection, or request for additional information
  10. Database Integration: Approved content published with attribution

Enhanced Review Timeline

  • Standard Review: 3-7 business days for complete submissions
  • Priority Review: 1-2 business days for critical community needs
  • Complex Review: 7-14 business days for disputed or unclear submissions
  • Expedited Process: Same-day review for emergency corrections
  • Seasonal Adjustment: Extended timelines during peak submission periods

Advanced Quality Control

  • AI-Assisted Screening: Automated checks for completeness and obvious errors
  • Expert Panel Review: Islamic scholars review questionable halal determinations
  • Community Peer Review: High-reputation users can provide additional verification
  • Continuous Monitoring: Regular audits of published content for accuracy
  • Feedback Integration: User reports of errors trigger re-review process

Strengthened Community Standards

Excellence Principles

  • Accuracy First: Prioritize correctness over speed of submission
  • Community Benefit: Consider how submission helps fellow Muslims
  • Comprehensive Detail: Provide thorough, complete information
  • Cultural Sensitivity: Respect diverse interpretations within Islamic jurisprudence
  • Continuous Improvement: Update information as circumstances change
  • Collaborative Spirit: Work with moderators to improve submissions

Advanced Community Responsibility

  • Error Reporting: Actively report outdated or incorrect information
  • Knowledge Sharing: Help other users understand halal requirements
  • Respectful Discourse: Maintain courteous communication about religious differences
  • Mentorship: Experienced users guide newcomers in proper submission practices
  • Local Expertise: Share knowledge of regional halal practices and certifications

Comprehensive Intellectual Property Protection

Enhanced User Rights

  • Content Ownership: You retain full ownership of original submitted content
  • Usage License: You grant Go Halal worldwide, perpetual license to use submissions
  • Attribution Rights: Request attribution for significant contributions
  • Modification Consent: We may edit for clarity, accuracy, or formatting
  • Withdrawal Process: Request removal of your submissions with valid justification

Strengthened Third-Party Protections

  • Copyright Compliance: Only submit original photos or properly licensed content
  • Trademark Respect: Accurately represent business names and branding
  • Photography Rights: Ensure legal right to photograph products and establishments
  • Business Consent: Obtain permission for interior restaurant photography when required
  • Fair Use Principles: Comply with fair use guidelines for commercial establishments

Robust Update Management

Proactive Information Maintenance

  • Change Notification: Alert us immediately of any status changes
  • Evidence Requirement: Provide documentation for significant updates
  • Verification Protocol: Major changes require same verification as new submissions
  • Version Control: Track changes over time for reference
  • Community Alerts: Notify users when important information changes

Systematic Database Maintenance

  • Regular Audits: Quarterly review of all database entries
  • Automated Monitoring: System alerts for potentially outdated information
  • Community-Driven Updates: User reporting system for changes
  • Seasonal Reviews: Special attention to temporary/seasonal establishments
  • Certification Tracking: Monitor expiry dates of halal certifications

Strengthened Accountability System

Graduated Response Framework

  • First Violation: Educational guidance and submission tutorial
  • Second Violation: Warning with specific improvement requirements
  • Third Violation: Temporary suspension (30 days) with mandatory training
  • Serious Violations: Immediate permanent ban with appeal process
  • Account Review: Quarterly assessment of user submission quality

Serious Violations Expanded

  • Intentional Misinformation: Deliberately false halal status claims
  • Religious Disrespect: Content offensive to Islamic principles
  • System Manipulation: Attempts to bypass review processes
  • Identity Fraud: Impersonating businesses or other users
  • Harassment: Targeting other community members
  • Commercial Exploitation: Using platform for unauthorized business promotion
