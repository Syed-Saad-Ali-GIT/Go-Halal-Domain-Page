Children's Privacy Policy for Go Halal

Last Updated: 31st July 2025

Overview

Go Halal is committed to protecting the privacy of children who use our mobile application. This Children's Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding children under 13 years of age, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Australian privacy laws.

General Approach to Children's Privacy

Age-Appropriate Design:

  • Go Halal is designed to be suitable for all ages, including children
  • The app provides educational information about halal food identification
  • No age verification is required as we don't collect personal information
  • Parents and guardians can supervise children's use of the app

Information We Collect from Children

What We DO Collect (Same as Adults)

Technical Information Only:

  • Device UUID: A unique identifier assigned to the device (not the child)
  • Location Data: Only when permission is granted by parent/guardian through device settings
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous app usage patterns through Amplitude Analytics
  • Camera Access: Only when permission is granted for barcode scanning

What We DO NOT Collect

No Personal Information:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Photos of children or personal images
  • Personal preferences or profiles
  • Social media information
  • School or family information
  • Any personally identifiable information

Parental Control and Consent

Device-Level Controls

Parents and guardians control children's app experience through:

  • Location Services: Enable/disable in device settings
  • Camera Access: Grant/deny permission in device settings
  • App Installation: Parent controls on app stores
  • Screen Time: Device-level usage controls

No Account Creation Required

  • Children cannot create accounts or profiles
  • No registration or sign-up process
  • No password or login requirements
  • No personal information requested at any time

How Children Can Use the App

Safe Features for Children

Educational Use:

  • Learn about halal food identification
  • Scan barcodes to check products
  • Find halal restaurants with family
  • Educational content about food choices

Supervised Activities:

  • Parents can help children scan products
  • Family restaurant discovery
  • Teaching moments about dietary choices
  • Building awareness of halal/non-halal distinctions

Features Children Cannot Access

Restricted Activities:

  • Cannot submit products or restaurants (no submission interface designed for children)
  • Cannot contact support independently
  • Cannot share personal information (no mechanism exists)
  • Cannot interact with other users

Data Usage and Sharing

How We Use Children's Data

Technical Data Only:

  • Device UUID: App functionality and preventing technical issues
  • Location: Finding nearby restaurants (when permitted)
  • Analytics: Improving app performance (anonymous data only)
  • Camera: Barcode scanning functionality (images not stored)

What We Don't Do

Never Share or Sell:

  • We never sell children's data
  • We never share personal information (we don't collect any)
  • We never use data for targeted advertising to children
  • We never create profiles based on children's usage

Third-Party Services and Children

Amplitude Analytics (Age-Neutral)

  • Collects anonymous usage data only
  • Cannot identify individual children
  • Data is aggregated and anonymized
  • Complies with child privacy standards

Future Advertising Considerations

Child-Safe Advertising (When Implemented):

  • No behavioral targeting of children
  • Age-appropriate content only
  • Compliance with advertising standards for children
  • Parental control options

Educational Value:

  • Provides learning opportunities about halal certification
  • Supports family discussions about dietary decisions

Data Retention for Children

Same as Adult Users

  • Device UUID: Retained while app is installed
  • Location Data: Not stored permanently, used for immediate searches only
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous data retained for 2 years (cannot identify individuals)
  • No Personal Data: Nothing personal to retain or delete

Automatic Deletion

  • All data automatically removed when app is uninstalled
  • No personal information stored that requires special deletion
  • Parents can delete all associated data by removing the app
